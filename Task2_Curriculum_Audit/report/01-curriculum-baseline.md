## 4. Initial audit observations

1. The listed curriculum contains foundational mathematics, programming, computer science and AI/ML subjects.
2. Data Structures, algorithms, databases, operating systems and networks provide a broad computer science foundation.
3. Machine Learning and Deep Learning appear as distinct subjects.
4. Data Wrangling and Visualization and Big Data Systems provide exposure to data-oriented work.
5. The page includes project, internship and thesis components in later semesters.
6. The visible course titles do not establish whether students learn model deployment, MLOps, cloud platforms, responsible AI, data privacy or large language model applications in sufficient depth.
7. Course titles alone cannot establish the depth, quality or practical hours of a subject.

## 5. Scope and limitations

This audit uses the supplied handbook page as its historical curriculum baseline. It does not claim to represent every regulation applicable to the 2023–2027 batch because the complete batch-specific regulation was not available.

Recommendations in the audit are proposals for consideration, not official changes to the programme.
